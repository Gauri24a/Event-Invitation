About the event

Grafest is the annual cultural festival of Graphic Era Deemed to be University. It is a vibrant celebration of creativity, talent, and youthful energy where students from across the state come together to compete, perform, and connect.

From showcasing your technical and cultural prowess in the afternoon to dancing your heart out at night with over 20,000 students packed into a massive stadium, Grafest is truly an experience of a lifetime. This three-day-long festival attracts participants from all over the state, along with celebrity performances that keep the crowd grooving till midnight.

Make sure you are part of this unforgettable journey and witness the magic of Grafest 2026 for real.
